About WhimsyLabs Virtual Lab

Major Grant Programs

Below are key grant programs that can fund ICT educational materials and science equipment. WhimsyLabs provides documentation support for all these programs.

🏛️

MEXT ICT Education Promotion

文部科学省 教育ICT活用推進事業

The Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) runs various programs to enhance education quality through ICT as part of the GIGA School initiative. These can fund digital teaching materials and teacher training.

EligiblePublic and private elementary, junior high, and high schools
Main UsesICT equipment, digital materials, teacher training

WhimsyLabs qualifies as 'digital teaching material' (デジタル教材). We provide educational effectiveness evidence for your application.

🔬

JSPS Grants-in-Aid (Kakenhi)

日本学術振興会 科学研究費助成事業（科研費）

Kakenhi, managed by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science, supports research activities at universities and technical colleges. It can fund research on teaching methods and educational material development.

EligibleUniversities, technical colleges, research institutions
Grant AmountVaries by category (¥100K - ¥10M+)

Can be used for research on science education effectiveness using simulation materials. We welcome collaborative research inquiries.

💻

GIGA School Related Subsidies

GIGAスクール構想関連補助金

Phase 2 of the GIGA School Program focuses not just on device renewal but also on enriching educational content. Various municipal subsidies are available.

EligibleMunicipal boards of education, public schools
Focus AreasDigital materials, learning systems, teacher training

Since WhimsyLabs runs on GIGA School standard devices, you can implement it with content costs only - no additional hardware budget needed.
