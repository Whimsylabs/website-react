## Tier 1 — write first

### 1. ✅ WRITTEN — Post 45 (`meta-quest-education-support-cliff-2030`, 2026-07-16, en-only) "Your school's VR headsets just lost their roadmap"
- **Hook:** From **20 Feb 2026** Meta stopped selling commercial Quest SKUs and Horizon Managed Services; HMS is free in maintenance mode with support only until **4 Jan 2030**. ([Meta help center](https://work.meta.com/help/1964851097790493/), [ManageXR](https://help.managexr.com/en/articles/13394353-update-meta-horizon-managed-services-will-be-free-starting-feb-20-2026), [RedboxVR](https://redboxvr.com/meta-horizon-managed-services-update/))
- **Why:** Schools that bought Quest fleets under "Meta for Education" now face a platform with no commercial roadmap and a 2030 support cliff — a procurement-risk story almost no education outlet has covered.
- **Angle:** Make lab software outlive the hardware — WhimsyLabs runs on Chromebooks AND VR, so labs aren't stranded when a headset vendor pivots. Natural follow-on to Post 44.

### 2. ✅ WRITTEN — Post 46 (`ai-chatbot-lawsuits-2026-schools`, 2026-07-16, en-only) The chatbot-harm legal reckoning (AI-safety spine post)
- **Hooks:** Character.AI/Google settled 5 wrongful-death/harm suits (**7 Jan 2026**, [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/01/07/business/character-ai-google-settle-teen-suicide-lawsuit)); Kentucky filed the first state-AG suit against a chatbot company (Jan 2026, [KY AG](https://www.kentucky.gov/Pages/Activity-stream.aspx?n=AttorneyGeneral&prId=1857)); Senate Judiciary advanced the GUARD Act banning AI companions for minors ([TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/lawsuits-exposed-how-chatbots-endanger-children-can-the-senates-new-bills-fix-it/)). ⚠️ A June 2026 stat ("1 in 5 US young people use chatbots for mental-health advice") has no identified publisher — drop or verify.
- **Why:** Safeguarding leads are told chatbots are a child-safety liability at the exact moment they're told to adopt AI.
- **Angle:** The cleanest fit yet for "no student chat window — safe by architecture." Frame around 2026's legal watershed, NOT a vendor checklist (see Post 43 feedback).

### 3. "How to respond to the science curriculum consultation" (publish late Aug / early Sept)
- **Hook:** DfE opens the National Curriculum + phase-one GCSE consultation **September 2026** (revised curriculum spring 2027, first teaching Sept 2028). Government accepted the recommendation to "more clearly articulate the purpose and expectations of high-quality practical work" and pilot "low-prep formats: labs, fieldwork." ([ASE update](https://www.ase.org.uk/news/england-national-curriculum-changes-update), [Gov response PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/690b2a4a14b040dfe82922ea/Government_response_to_the_Curriculum_and_Assessment_Review.pdf), [Schools Week](https://schoolsweek.co.uk/curriculum-review-the-subject-specific-policy-proposals/))
- **Why:** Every science lead in England will be asked to respond within weeks of returning in September; "what counts as high-quality practical work" is a live policy question.
- **Angle:** Simulated practicals as the low-prep, high-frequency route to the practical-work expectations DfE just accepted.

### 4. ✅ WRITTEN — Post 47 (`neu-survey-ai-critical-thinking-decline`, 2026-07-16, en-only) "Productivity without learning" — NEU survey × OECD outlook
- **Hooks:** NEU State of Education (**April 2026**, 9,408 teachers): 76% now use AI (from 53%), 49% report no school AI policy, **66% of secondary teachers say pupil critical thinking has declined** ([NEU](https://neu.org.uk/latest/press-releases/state-education-2026-ai) ⚠️ page blocked fetching — verify exact figures). OECD Digital Education Outlook (**19 Jan 2026**): GenAI lifts task completion ~48% but performance drops ~17% when the AI is removed ([OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-digital-education-outlook-2026_062a7394-en.html) ⚠️ verify figures in the report PDF).
- **Angle:** AI that makes students *do* the thinking (hands-on lab actions, no answer-generating chat) is the counter-model to critical-thinking decline. "Doing vs producing."

### 5. ✅ WRITTEN — Post 48 (`us-federal-ai-education-grant-priority-2026`, 2026-07-16, en-only) "How to write AI into your US federal grant application" (US market)
- **Hook:** US Dept of Education final rule (**published 14 Apr 2026, effective 13 May 2026**): grant applications prioritised for ethical AI use, AI literacy, special-education AI, and AI-driven personalised learning. ([K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/how-the-education-department-will-prioritize-ai-in-awarding-grants/817340/), [ai.gov/education](https://www.ai.gov/initiatives/education))
- **Angle:** Checklist post — safe-by-architecture AI tutoring and SEND support map directly onto the rule's priority language. Serves the under-covered US market; pairs with the Title IV-A / Perkins V grant guides.

### 8. "Does your science software pass the 2026 state AI laws?" (US)
- **Hooks:** 134 AI-in-education bills across 31 states this session ([MultiState, 9 Apr 2026](https://www.multistate.us/insider/2026/4/9/how-states-are-regulating-ai-in-education-this-legislative-session)); Illinois statewide guidance deadline **1 July 2026** ([WAND](https://www.wandtv.com/news/statehouse/il-law-requires-guidance-for-ai-in-elementary-secondary-education/article_156c54e8-b45f-4d56-8ee8-bb047e3a4ff7.html)); Oklahoma SB 1734 requires educator supervision + bans AI in high-stakes decisions; NYC preliminary rules **24 Mar 2026** ([Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/newyork/2026/03/24/preliminary-ai-policy-nyc-schools/)).
- **Angle:** Teacher-surfaced (not student-facing) AI passes the human-oversight tests by design. 50-state trend explainer.

### 9. EU AI Act delay explainer (es/fr/de audiences)
- **Hook:** May 2026 Omnibus agreement postpones Annex III high-risk obligations (incl. AI evaluating learning outcomes / monitoring students) from 2 Aug 2026 to **2 Dec 2027**; the emotion-inference ban on students and the AI-literacy duty stand. ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/), [Ogletree](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/eu-ai-act-amended-parliament-votes-to-delay-key-deadlines/)) ⚠️ Confirm final formal adoption of the Omnibus before publishing.
- **Angle:** "The EU gave schools 16 extra months on high-risk AI — use them to choose tools compliant by design." Teacher-in-the-loop oversight is exactly what Annex III requires. Strong candidate for full es/fr/de translation.

### 10. Ofqual and the AI-marking debate (careful — near the saturated lane)
- **Hook:** Ofqual working paper **14 Jan 2026**: AI cannot be sole marker; AQA CEO says only "public confidence" stops machine marking. ([Ofqual blog](https://ofqual.blog.gov.uk/2026/01/14/using-ai-in-marking-why-technical-capability-fairness-and-transparency-all-matter/), [Schools Week](https://schoolsweek.co.uk/ai-marking-the-answer-to-poor-recruitment-or-pure-snake-oil/))
- **Angle:** Flip it — not *who* marks but *what* gets marked: rich process data from lab actions gives human markers better evidence than a final written answer. **Skip if the draft drifts into existing process-based-assessment territory.**
