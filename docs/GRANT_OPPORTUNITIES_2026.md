### 🔴 Urgent (next 3 weeks)

#### Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation: Earlier identification of children with SEN — TOP PICK
- **Closes: Wed 5 August 2026, 11:00** (opened 23 June; projects start 1 Oct 2026)
- **£200,000–£500,000 per project (inc. VAT), 100% funded** — a contract, not a matched grant. Up to 12 winners; Phase 1 of a potential 3-phase competition.
- Eligibility: any size organisation, single applicant or with subcontractors; majority of work in the UK.
- **Pitch angle:** WhimsyCat's action-based inference (no chat window) generates rich process data from real lab tasks — a safeguarding-safe way to surface early SEND indicators (motor skills, sequencing, hesitation, language-independent problem-solving) that written assessments miss.
- https://apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk/competition/2511/overview/cc9f4bb9-55a5-4b4c-9843-015c385e021f

#### DfE EdTech Testbed Programme — register now (free)
- 4-year, £23m programme with EEF (£7m for up to 15 RCTs; Stage 1 = up to 100 rapid evaluations).
- Not cash — **free/subsidised independent efficacy evidence**, the most valuable asset for UK school procurement and every future bid. Focus areas: workload reduction, outcomes, inclusion — all three fit.
- **Action: email Edtech.TESTBED@education.gov.uk to register as a supplier.**
- https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/news/partnership-department-for-education-impact-of-edtech-tools

## Part 3 — Awards, events, credibility

### Award deadlines
- **3 Aug 2026** — [Teach Awards](https://www.teachawards.com/) (Primary/Secondary/Early Years). Low effort, teacher-judged, covered in Teach magazines.
- **15 Sept 2026** — GESA (free; finals at Bett).
- **25 Sept 2026** — Bett Awards 2027 (AI in Education, SEND, Innovation sub-£3m turnover categories).
- **~Aug/Sept ⚠️** — [GESS Education Awards](https://www.gessdubai.com/awards/) (ceremony 11 Nov, Dubai; entry fee currently waived; deadline unpublished — ask organisers).
- **Late 2026/early 2027** — [EdTech 50 (BusinessCloud)](https://businesscloud.co.uk/edtech-50-uks-most-innovative-education-tech-creators-for-2026/) nominations. Very low effort.

### Events
- **18–19 Nov 2026** — [Schools & Academies Show](https://www.schoolsandacademiesshow.co.uk/), NEC Birmingham. MAT-level decision-makers — the right room for multi-school subscriptions and Inclusive-Mainstream-Fund spend conversations.
- **7–9 Jan 2027** — [ASE Annual Conference](https://www.ase.org.uk/events/2027-ase-annual-conference-shaping-future-of-science-education-together), Northampton. **Best audience-fit event of the year** — densest concentration of science teachers/technicians, and the science programme-of-study drafting teams (ASE/RSB/RSC/IOP) will be there between the Sept 2026 consultation and final curriculum. Aim for a stand + workshop.
- **20–22 Jan 2027** — [Bett UK](https://uk.bettshow.com/), ExCeL. Stand is expensive; cheaper routes = GESA finals stage, Bett Awards shortlist, speaking submissions.
- **12–14 May 2027** — [EDIX Tokyo](https://www.edix-expo.jp/hub/en-gb.html) (JETRO support available for foreign exhibitors). The venue for GIGA "utilisation-year" software.
- **27–30 June 2027** — ISTELive 27, Boston. Main US buyer show; pair with ISTE Seal.
- ⚠️ EDUtech Europe 2026 (Amsterdam, Oct) — conflicting listings; confirm the European edition is running before budgeting.

### Credibility & media
- **[ISTE Seal](https://iste.org/iste-seal)** — rolling, 4–6 week review; only ~15% of EdTech Index products hold evidence certification. Fastest formal credibility badge for the US market.
- **[EEF EdTech research-agenda theme](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/projects-and-evaluation/research-agenda-themes-priority-areas/research-agenda-theme-edtech)** — two funding rounds/year; multi-year commitment but an EEF-evaluated efficacy claim is unmatched in UK procurement.
- **Media hooks (low effort, high value):** DfE "AI Tutoring Tool Pioneers" (Jan 2026, safe AI tutoring for 450k FSM pupils); NEU April 2026 survey (66% of secondary teachers say AI hurt critical thinking; only 14% back government AI tutors) — pitch the "no chat window, learning by doing" counter-narrative to Schools Week/TES education correspondents. EngineeringUK practical-science data (fortnightly practicals 44%→26%, 2016→2023) as the standing stat for op-eds.
- **[The Edtech Podcast](https://theedtechpodcast.com/)** (UK, 2k+ downloads/week) and US podcast circuit — pitch the "AI tutor with no chat window" angle. Very low effort.
- **ASE journals** (School Science Review, Education in Science, Primary Science) accept practitioner articles — byline a science-teacher user rather than the company.
- **CLEAPSS / SSERC** — no formal vendor programme; value is alignment and informal dialogue on simulation-as-preparation-for-real-practicals. Technicians are a genuine gatekeeper audience.
