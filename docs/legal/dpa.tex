\begin{center}
{\Large\bfseries DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT}

\vspace{0.5cm}

{\small Effective: February 2026}
\end{center}

\vspace{1cm}

This Data Processing Agreement (``DPA'') forms part of and is incorporated into the service agreement (the ``Agreement'') between WhimsyLabs Ltd (``WhimsyLabs'', ``Processor'') and the Customer (``Customer'', ``Controller'') for the provision of virtual laboratory educational services (the ``Services'').

The parties acknowledge and agree that with regard to the Processing of Customer Personal Data, Customer is the Controller and WhimsyLabs is the Processor acting on behalf of Customer as further described in Annex A (Details of Processing).

% ============================================================
\section{Definitions}
% ============================================================

For the purposes of this DPA, the following definitions shall apply:

\begin{description}[style=nextline, leftmargin=1cm, font=\normalfont]
    \item[``Customer Personal Data''] means the categories of Personal Data set out in Annex A that are Processed by WhimsyLabs on behalf of the Customer in connection with the Services.
    
    \item[``Data Protection Laws''] means: (i) Regulation 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (General Data Protection Regulation or ``GDPR''); (ii) the GDPR as it forms part of domestic law in the United Kingdom by virtue of the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018 and the Data Protection Act 2018 (``UK GDPR''); and (iii) any other applicable data protection legislation in any relevant jurisdiction, each as amended or replaced from time to time.
    
    \item[``Personnel''] means any current, former, or prospective employee, consultant, contractor, or other personnel of either party.
    
    \item[``SCCs''] means the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for data transfers between EU and non-EU countries, and where applicable, the International Data Transfer Addendum published by the UK Information Commissioner's Office.
    
    \item[``Sensitive Data''] means Personal Data revealing racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, health data, data concerning sex life or sexual orientation, or data relating to criminal convictions and offences.
    
    \item[``Sub-processor''] means any third party engaged by WhimsyLabs to Process Customer Personal Data on behalf of the Customer.
    
    \item[``Controller'', ``Data Subject'', ``Personal Data'', ``Personal Data Breach'', ``Processing'', ``Processor''] shall have the meanings given to them in the GDPR.
    
    \item[``Supervisory Authority''] means the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the United Kingdom, or any other competent data protection authority with jurisdiction over the Processing activities.
\end{description}

% ============================================================
\section{Scope and Roles}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Roles of the Parties}
The parties acknowledge and agree that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item Customer is the Controller of Customer Personal Data;
    \item WhimsyLabs is a Processor acting on behalf of Customer; and
    \item WhimsyLabs shall Process Customer Personal Data only in accordance with Customer's documented instructions and the terms of this DPA.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Processing Instructions}
In the course of providing the Services, WhimsyLabs shall Process Customer Personal Data only:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item in accordance with Customer's documented lawful instructions as set forth in this DPA and the Agreement;
    \item to the extent necessary for the performance of the Services; or
    \item as required by applicable law, in which case WhimsyLabs shall inform Customer in advance of such Processing to the maximum extent permitted by law.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Customer Obligations}
The Customer shall:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item comply with its obligations under Data Protection Laws in respect of its Processing of Customer Personal Data;
    \item ensure that all Processing instructions issued to WhimsyLabs are lawful;
    \item provide all notices and obtain all consents necessary under Data Protection Laws for WhimsyLabs to Process Customer Personal Data for the purposes described in this DPA; and
    \item not provide any Sensitive Data to WhimsyLabs unless explicitly agreed in writing.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Sensitive Data}
The Customer shall not provide (or cause to be provided) any Sensitive Data to WhimsyLabs for Processing under the Agreement. WhimsyLabs shall have no liability for any Sensitive Data provided in breach of this provision.

% ============================================================
\section{Security}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Security Measures}
WhimsyLabs shall implement and maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Customer Personal Data from accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access, as set out in Annex B (Security Measures).

\subsection{Confidentiality}
WhimsyLabs shall:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item ensure that all Personnel authorized to Process Customer Personal Data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations;
    \item take reasonable steps to ensure the reliability of Personnel who have access to Customer Personal Data; and
    \item ensure that Customer Personal Data is Processed only by Personnel who require access for the performance of the Services.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Security Updates}
WhimsyLabs may update or modify its security measures from time to time, provided that such updates do not materially decrease the overall level of protection afforded to Customer Personal Data.

% ============================================================
\section{Sub-processing}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Authorized Sub-processors}
Customer grants WhimsyLabs general authorization to engage Sub-processors to Process Customer Personal Data, subject to the terms of this Section 4. A list of current Sub-processors is set out in Annex C.

\subsection{Sub-processor Obligations}
WhimsyLabs shall:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item enter into a written agreement with each Sub-processor imposing data protection obligations no less protective than those set out in this DPA;
    \item remain fully liable to Customer for the acts and omissions of its Sub-processors; and
    \item maintain an up-to-date list of Sub-processors on its website at \url{https://whimsylabs.ai/data-security}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{New Sub-processors}
WhimsyLabs shall notify Customer at least thirty (30) days in advance of engaging any new Sub-processor by updating the list on its website. Customer may object to a new Sub-processor by providing written notice within fourteen (14) days of being notified. The parties shall negotiate in good faith to resolve any objection.

% ============================================================
\section{Data Subject Rights}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Assistance with Requests}
Taking into account the nature of the Processing, WhimsyLabs shall assist Customer by appropriate technical and organizational measures in fulfilling Customer's obligation to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Data Protection Laws.

\subsection{Response Timeline}
WhimsyLabs shall promptly notify Customer upon receiving any request from a Data Subject and shall respond to Customer's requests for assistance within five (5) business days.

\subsection{No Direct Response}
WhimsyLabs shall not respond directly to any Data Subject request unless authorized by Customer or required by applicable law.

% ============================================================
\section{Personal Data Breach}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Notification}
WhimsyLabs shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

\subsection{Information to be Provided}
Such notification shall include, to the extent known:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item a description of the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and records concerned;
    \item the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach;
    \item the measures taken or proposed to address the Personal Data Breach; and
    \item the name and contact details of a point of contact from whom further information may be obtained.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Cooperation}
WhimsyLabs shall cooperate with Customer and take reasonable steps to assist in the investigation, mitigation, and remediation of the Personal Data Breach.

% ============================================================
\section{Data Protection Impact Assessments}
% ============================================================

WhimsyLabs shall provide reasonable assistance to Customer with any data protection impact assessments or prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities that Customer is required to undertake under Data Protection Laws, taking into account the nature of the Processing and the information available to WhimsyLabs.

% ============================================================
\section{International Transfers}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Location of Processing}
WhimsyLabs shall Process Customer Personal Data within the United Kingdom and European Economic Area unless otherwise specified in Annex A or agreed in writing.

\subsection{Transfer Mechanisms}
In the event that any international transfer of Customer Personal Data is required, WhimsyLabs shall ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with Data Protection Laws, including:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item transfers to countries subject to an adequacy decision;
    \item transfers pursuant to the SCCs; or
    \item other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.
\end{enumerate}

% ============================================================
\section{Audit Rights}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Information and Access}
WhimsyLabs shall make available to Customer all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Customer or an auditor mandated by Customer.

\subsection{Audit Conditions}
Any audit shall be subject to:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
    \item at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice;
    \item execution during normal business hours;
    \item auditors being bound by confidentiality obligations; and
    \item reasonable measures to minimize disruption to WhimsyLabs' operations.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Third-Party Certifications}
Customer acknowledges that WhimsyLabs may satisfy audit requests by providing relevant third-party certifications, audit reports, or other documentation demonstrating compliance.

% ============================================================
\section{Return and Deletion of Data}
% ============================================================

\subsection{Data Export}
Upon termination of the Agreement, or upon Customer's request, WhimsyLabs shall provide Customer with the ability to export Customer Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.

\subsection{Deletion}
Subject to Section 10.3, WhimsyLabs shall delete all Customer Personal Data within thirty (30) days following termination of the Agreement or receipt of a deletion request, and shall provide written confirmation of deletion upon request.

\subsection{Retention Requirements}
WhimsyLabs may retain Customer Personal Data to the extent required by applicable law, provided that WhimsyLabs shall ensure the confidentiality of such data and shall Process it only for the purposes required by law.

% ============================================================
\section{Liability}
% ============================================================

Each party's liability under this DPA shall be subject to the limitations and exclusions set out in the Agreement. Notwithstanding the foregoing, nothing in this DPA shall limit either party's liability for breaches of Data Protection Laws to the extent such limitation is prohibited by applicable law.

% ============================================================
\section{Term}
% ============================================================

This DPA shall come into effect on the effective date of the Agreement and shall remain in effect for the duration of the Agreement. The obligations of WhimsyLabs under this DPA shall survive termination of the Agreement to the extent necessary to complete the deletion or return of Customer Personal Data.

% ============================================================

% ============================================================

\subsection{Governing Law}
This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

\subsection{Jurisdiction}
The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection with this DPA.

\subsection{Severability}
If any provision of this DPA is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

\subsection{Entire Agreement}
This DPA, together with the Agreement and any applicable SCCs, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.

\section*{Annex B: Security Measures}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Annex B: Security Measures}
% ============================================================

WhimsyLabs implements and maintains the following technical and organizational security measures:

\subsection*{B.1 Technical Measures}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Encryption in Transit:} All data transmitted over public networks is encrypted using TLS 1.3 or higher.
    
    \item \textbf{Encryption at Rest:} All stored data is encrypted using AES-256 encryption.
    
    \item \textbf{Access Controls:} Role-based access control (RBAC) implementing the principle of least privilege.
    
    \item \textbf{Authentication:} Multi-factor authentication (MFA) required for all administrative access.
    
    \item \textbf{Network Security:} Firewalls, intrusion detection and prevention systems, and DDoS mitigation.
    
    \item \textbf{Logging and Monitoring:} Comprehensive logging of access and security events with real-time monitoring and alerting.
    
    \item \textbf{Backup and Recovery:} Automated encrypted backups with geographically redundant storage and tested recovery procedures.
    
    \item \textbf{Vulnerability Management:} Regular vulnerability scanning and patch management procedures.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{B.2 Organizational Measures}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Personnel Security:} Background checks for employees with access to Personal Data; confidentiality agreements.
    
    \item \textbf{Security Training:} Regular security awareness training for all personnel.
    
    \item \textbf{Access Management:} Formal procedures for granting, modifying, and revoking access rights.
    
    \item \textbf{Incident Response:} Documented incident response procedures with defined roles and escalation paths.
    
    \item \textbf{Security Testing:} Annual penetration testing and security audits conducted by qualified third parties.
    
    \item \textbf{Business Continuity:} Business continuity and disaster recovery plans with regular testing.
    
    \item \textbf{Vendor Management:} Due diligence and contractual requirements for Sub-processors.
\end{enumerate}
