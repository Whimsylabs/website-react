Data Processing Agreement

Version 1.0 | Effective Date: February 2026

1. Parties

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between:

Data Processor: WhimsyLabs Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, with registered office in Edinburgh, United Kingdom ("WhimsyLabs", "we", "us")

Data Controller: The educational institution, school, or organization subscribing to WhimsyLabs services ("School", "you", "Controller")

2. Definitions

  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • "Student Data" means Personal Data relating to students enrolled at the School.
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data.
  • "Data Protection Laws" means UK GDPR, GDPR, and any applicable data protection legislation.

3. Scope and Purpose of Processing

WhimsyLabs processes Personal Data solely for the purpose of providing virtual laboratory educational services, including:

  • User authentication and account management
  • Delivering personalized learning experiences
  • Recording educational progress and assessment results
  • Providing AI-powered tutoring and feedback
  • Technical support and service improvement

4. Categories of Data Processed

Category Data Elements Purpose
Identity Data Name, username, school ID Account creation, identification
Contact Data Email address (institutional) Communications, password recovery
Educational Data Experiment results, progress, grades Learning delivery, assessment
Technical Data IP address, device type, browser Service delivery, troubleshooting

5. Data Processor Obligations

WhimsyLabs agrees to:

  • Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller
  • Ensure persons authorized to process data are bound by confidentiality
  • Implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures
  • Not engage sub-processors without prior written authorization
  • Assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests
  • Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of services
  • Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance

6. Security Measures

WhimsyLabs implements the following security measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrative access
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Access controls based on principle of least privilege
  • Automated backup systems with encryption
  • Incident response procedures with 72-hour breach notification

7. Sub-Processors

WhimsyLabs uses the following sub-processors:

Sub-Processor Purpose Location
Amazon Web Services (AWS) Cloud hosting infrastructure EU (Ireland)
Cloudflare CDN and security services EU/UK endpoints

8. Data Subject Rights

WhimsyLabs will assist the Controller in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Data Protection Laws, including:

  • Right of access
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure (subject to retention requirements)
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal Data will be retained for the duration of the service agreement plus 90 days. Upon termination or request:

  • The School may export all data in a standard format
  • WhimsyLabs will delete all Personal Data within 30 days
  • Deletion will be confirmed in writing

10. International Transfers

WhimsyLabs processes all data within the UK and European Economic Area. Any international transfers will only occur with appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions).

11. Audit Rights

The Controller may audit WhimsyLabs' compliance with this DPA upon reasonable notice. WhimsyLabs will provide access to relevant documentation and systems.

12. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA shall be subject to the limitations set out in the main service agreement.

13. Term and Termination

This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the service agreement. Data processing obligations survive termination until all Personal Data is deleted or returned.
