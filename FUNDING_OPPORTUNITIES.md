# Funding Opportunities for WhimsyLabs
**Last Updated: November 2025**

## Overview
WhimsyLabs is an award-winning virtual laboratory simulation platform with a non-profit, equity-focused mission. This document outlines funding sources aligned with the company's goals of democratizing STEM education.

---

## UK Government & Public Funding

### 1. **Innovate UK Smart Grants**
- **Amount**: £25,000 - £2 million
- **Focus**: R&D projects in innovative technology
- **Fit**: VR/AR development, AI tutoring systems
- **Deadline**: Rolling applications, quarterly reviews
- **URL**: innovateuk.ukri.org

### 2. **UKRI EdTech Research Grants**
- **Amount**: £100,000 - £1 million
- **Focus**: Education technology research and impact studies
- **Fit**: Evidence-based studies on virtual lab effectiveness
- **Deadline**: Annual calls (typically March/September)

### 3. **Department for Education (DfE) EdTech Innovation Fund**
- **Amount**: Varies (£50,000 - £500,000)
- **Focus**: Scalable edtech solutions addressing educational inequality
- **Fit**: Perfect alignment with equity mission
- **Deadline**: Annual competitions

### 4. **Scottish Enterprise R&D Grants**
- **Amount**: Up to £100,000
- **Focus**: Scottish businesses developing innovative products
- **Fit**: Based in Edinburgh, already engaged with Scottish ecosystem
- **Deadline**: Rolling applications

### 5. **Nesta Challenges**
- **Amount**: £50,000 - £300,000

- **Fit**: Addressing STEM skills gap, equity focus
- **Deadline**: Various challenge-specific deadlines

## Funding Strategy Tips

### Strengthen Your Application

- **Evidence Base**: Conduct and publish impact studies showing learning outcomes
- **Partnerships**: Build consortiums with universities and schools for larger grants
- **Geographic Focus**: Leverage Scottish base while demonstrating UK-wide impact
- **Awards**: Continue entering competitions to build credibility
- **Testimonials**: Collect quantitative and qualitative evidence from educators

### Key Messaging for Funders

1. **Educational Equity**: Addressing STEM gap in underfunded schools
2. **Evidence of Impact**: BETT award, educator testimonials, usage data
3. **Innovation**: Only platform combining high-fidelity physics + AI + VR
4. **Scalability**: Web-based, multi-device accessibility
5. **Mission-Driven**: Non-profit model focused on access, not profit
6. **Curriculum Alignment**: Directly supports UK national curriculum
7. **Timely**: Addresses UK's declining STEM rankings (27th in OECD)

### Application Timing

- **Q1 (Jan-Mar)**: Nuffield Foundation, DfE EdTech Fund, various competitions
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun)**: UKRI calls, Erasmus+, GESA submissions
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep)**: Foundation planning for next year, autumn grant rounds
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Horizon Europe calls, corporate giving programmes
